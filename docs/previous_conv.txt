Think of it like this:

The manual is your playbook.
The engine is your worker.
The playbook says:

how to speak
what to prioritize
what “good output” looks like
The worker does:

reads customer conversation
follows those rules
gives you the result in the app
Simple real-life analogy:

Manual = recipe
Engine = chef
App screen = finished dish served to you
Flow:

You define rules in the playbook.
You copy those rules into the worker’s behavior.
Worker uses those rules every time someone clicks AI.
Result appears in Conversations.
So both are needed:

Without the manual, behavior gets random over time.
Without the engine, the manual is just notes and nothing happens.
