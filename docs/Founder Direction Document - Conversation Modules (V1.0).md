

## Founder Direction Document
## Feature: Customer Interaction & Intelligence Engine (formerly Conversation
## Modules)
## Version: Founder Alignment Draft
- Problem Statement (Why This Exists)
Today, sales teams experience communication chaos.
What’s happening:
● Conversations with customers happen across many platforms:
## ○ Messenger
## ○ Viber
○ WhatsApp
## ○ Email
## ○ SMS
○ Phone calls
● Salespeople must:
○ Jump between multiple apps
○ Manually track conversations
○ Rely on memory or spreadsheets
The result:
❌ Messages are missed
❌ Follow-ups are delayed or forgotten
❌ Customer history is incomplete
❌ Context is lost when salespeople change
❌ Managers have low visibility
❌ Deals are lost due to poor coordination
Core Problem (Simplified)
“We do not have a reliable system to track, manage, and understand
customer relationships across all communication channels.”
- Goal (What We Are Actually Building)
We are not building a messaging tool.
We are building a Relationship Intelligence System.

## Primary Goal
To create a system that captures every customer interaction, preserves
relationship context, and helps sales teams consistently manage and
close deals.
Specifically, the system should:
## 1. Capture Everything
● Every conversation
● Every call
● Every meeting
● Across all channels
 Nothing is lost
- Create a Complete Customer Memory
● Full history of interactions
● Organized in one place
● Easy to understand
 Anyone can pick up the relationship
- Prevent Missed Follow-Ups
● Track next steps
● Remind users what to do
● Ensure no opportunity is forgotten
 This directly impacts revenue
## 4. Enable Team Scalability
● Salespeople can change
● Context is preserved
● Customers are still handled properly
 Business does not depend on individuals
- Prepare for AI (Future)
● Record structured data
● Understand behavior patterns
● Enable recommendations

 This becomes “relationship intelligence”
- Simple Explanation (For Clarity)
Think of the system as:
 A smart notebook for every customer
## Where:
● Every interaction is saved
● Anyone can understand the story
● It reminds you what to do next
● It helps you build better relationships
- System Architecture (How We Should Build It)
We should not think in features like “Inbox” or “Phone”.
We should build in layers:
Layer 1 — Data Capture (Foundation)
## Capture:
● Messages (all platforms)
## ● Calls
## ● Meetings
## ● Notes
 Rule: If it happens, it must be recorded
Layer 2 — Customer Timeline (Memory)
## Create:
● One unified timeline per customer
 This is the core product
Layer 3 — Sales Execution (Action)
## Enable:
## ● Follow-ups
● Next steps

## ● Reminders
## ● Ownership
 This drives actual sales results
Layer 4 — Intelligence Layer (Understanding)
## Store:

## ● Objections
## ● Insights
## ● Tags
Layer 5 — AI Layer (Future)
## Eventually:
## ● Recommendations
## ● Predictions
## ● Automation
