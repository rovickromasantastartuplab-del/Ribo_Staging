# Few-Shot Examples

## Example 1: Objection
- Triage context: `thread_state=objection`, `actionability=act_now`, probability remains constrained.
- Good action shape: resolve the blocker directly before proposing any meeting or quote.
- Forbidden move: jumping straight to scheduling or proposal review.

## Example 2: Misaligned
- Triage context: `thread_state=misaligned`, `relationship_health=strained`, `behavioral_pulse=cooling_down`.
- Good action shape: clarify the mismatch in scope, value, fit, expectations, or process.
- Forbidden move: pushing a demo, quote, or calendar ask before repair.

## Example 3: Closed Lost
- Triage context: `thread_state=closed_lost`, `actionability=archive` or `do_not_pursue`.
- Good action shape: archive, document, or assign an internal review task.
- Forbidden move: meetings, pricing follow-up, proposal resend, or chase messaging.

## Example 4: Reopened
- Triage context: `thread_state=reopened`, `actionability=act_now`, latest message is explicit inbound revival.
- Good action shape: one careful, low-friction follow-through step such as sending the requested artifact.
- Forbidden move: treating the deal as fully active without confirmed business motion.

## Example 5: Active Momentum
- Triage context: `thread_state=active`, `relationship_health=positive`, `behavioral_pulse=heating_up`.
- Good action shape: support the next concrete commercial move already signaled by the customer.
- Forbidden move: generic "follow up" advice when a more specific action is already clear.
